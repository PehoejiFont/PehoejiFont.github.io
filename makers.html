Adding POJ to a font

A checklist for designers and developers. The full technical report has the details, a feature-code sketch and the survey data.

1. Characters to add

CharacterWhat for
U+0358  ͘the o͘ dot (zero-width mark)
U+030D  ̍8th tone
U+207F ⁿ, U+1D3A ᴺnasal, and its all-caps form
U+030B, U+030CKPL 9th and 6th tones
ı U+0131, ȷ U+0237dotless forms for i̍
ḿ Ḿ ǹ Ǹ ń Ń ň Ň ŏ Ŏ ő ű ǎ ě ǐ ǒ ǔ…ready-made letters, so no app has to borrow them

2. Snap points (anchors)

  • top on every letter that takes a tone: a e ı o u m n and capitals.
  • topright on o O and on every toned o: ó ò ô ǒ ō ŏ ő and capitals. Most fonts miss some of these, because after text is tidied to NFC the dot follows ó, not o.
  • Check wide accents and bold weights: the dot must not touch the tone or the next letter (o͘h, o͘ⁿ).

3. Rules

  1. Build POJ letters in ccmp, never in liga. liga is switched off by CSS letter-spacing and in Word.
  2. If you add ready-drawn letters, match all three input forms: ó + 0358, o 0301 0358 and o 0358 0301. List longer sequences first.
  3. Keep the anchors even when you have ready-drawn letters, as a safety net.
  4. Always ship a GPOS mark feature. Browsers only guess positions when a font has none, and other engines don't guess at all.
  5. Swap i → ı before above-marks in ccmp.
  6. Use standard glyph names (uni0358, o_uni0358) so text copied from PDFs comes out right.
  7. Give the font enough height: Ó͘ Ő M̍ are taller than Western accents and get clipped otherwise.
  8. Don't let -- turn into a dash.

4. Test

This repository has three small scripts (Python, needs fonttools, uharfbuzz, pillow):

tools/poj_font_audit.py  Font.ttf   # which POJ characters, features and anchors exist
tools/poj_shape_test.py  Font.ttf   # how each POJ letter is built, NFC vs decomposed
tools/poj_specimen.py    out/ Font.ttf   # picture to check by eye

Then look at the test line in Chrome, Safari and Word, once with letter-spacing, and in the Google Fonts version if you publish there.

Help fix it upstream

  • googlefonts/glyphsets#5 asks Google Fonts to require U+0358, U+030D and U+207F in its Latin set.
  • googlefonts/lang has no entry for Taigi in Latin script yet. Adding one would let Google's tools check fonts for POJ.
