Every script, raw dataset, open research question, and peer-review verification record behind the PehoejiFont documentation and fork effort.

Rebuilding the site’s GenYo Min font subsets

This site self-hosts cut-down Regular and Bold copies of 源樣明體 GenYo Min (assets/fonts/GenYoMin2TW-R-site.woff2 and GenYoMin2TW-B-site.woff2). They include all Latin letters, POJ/KPL combining marks, GenYo’s ccmp precomposed o͘ glyphs, and every Han character appearing in *.html. After adding new Han characters to any page, rebuild with:

shell — Rebuild site font subsets
tools/build_site_fonts.sh <folder-with-GenYoMin2TW-R-and-B.otf>

Note: GenYo Min has no standalone dotless ı (U+0131) or ȷ (U+0237) in its cmap, so those two standalone symbols on the Font Maker’s Guide fall back to a system font; running POJ text like i̍ is unaffected because GenYo’s precomposed ccmp glyph still omits the dot.

Open research questions

  1. Full CoreText & DirectWrite matrix: Complete cross-engine testing on macOS (CoreText) and Windows (DirectWrite) for mark reordering, NFD recomposition, missing precomposed fallback, and unanchored U+0358.
  2. Google Fonts cross-slice splitting: Test whether o + U+0358 in Iansui and Huninn can split across the latin and Taigi unicode-range slices in Chrome, Safari, and Firefox.
  3. Untested community fonts: Download and run the audit pipeline on Caivun, Kaukhia, and Indeciso.
  4. Legacy encoding prevalence: Scan real corpora (such as ChhoeTaigi datasets and older Taigi web archives) to quantify how common middle-dot, Vietnamese horn, PUA, and 8-bit encodings remain in the wild.

Peer-review & verification log (Gemini 4)

The initial draft of docs/poj-font-implementation.md was independently reviewed by two Gemini 4 XHigh models (gemini-4-neon-xhigh and gemini-4-hydrogen-xhigh) via headless agy without tool access; full transcripts live in docs/reviews/.

Confirmed independently by both reviews

  • Canonical Combining Class values (ccc=230 vs ccc=232) and canonical reordering behaviour;
  • The Unicode 17.0 precomposed POJ/KPL table;
  • NFKC destruction of U+207F (ⁿ → n);
  • Soft_Dotted dotless-i requirement for sequences like i̍;
  • HarfBuzz canonical reordering before GSUB;
  • CSS disabling optional liga under non-zero letter-spacing.

Unverified claims & rejected items

  • Flagged as unverified reviewer claims: DirectWrite composition/reordering/fallback behaviour; Microsoft Word’s ligature default; corpus frequency of extra legacy PUA/8-bit/look-alike encodings.
  • Rejected or softened: (1) “A 2-glyph ligature listed first permanently shadows a 3-glyph one” — feaLib and makeotf sort ligatures longest-first within a lookup (Rule 2 still advises writing longest-first for portability across compilers). (2) NunitoPOJ dotless-i concern — verified that with liga disabled, NunitoPOJ still outputs dotlessi + uni030D via ccmp.
