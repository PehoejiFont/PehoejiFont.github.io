Live POJ Test Bench

Test any font on your computer or in your browser across NFC, NFD, and non-canonical reverse mark order, toggle letter-spacing to expose liga bugs, and inspect every underlying Unicode codepoint live.

Interactive tester & codepoint inspector

6-point visual checklist

After switching the preview font above (or pasting into Word, Pages, Illustrator, or your editor), check all six items:

  1. No empty boxes (□) and no single letters borrowed from a mismatched fallback font (watch ḿ ǹ, the o͘ dot, and the 8th-tone line).
  2. The o͘ dot sits cleanly at the top right of every o — especially capitals O͘ Ó͘ Ō͘ O̍͘ and wide accents ō͘ ô͘ ŏ͘.
  3. i̍ drops its dot cleanly under the 8th-tone vertical line (why this matters).
  4. The 8th-tone vertical line centres cleanly over a̍ e̍ o̍ u̍ m̍ n̍ and capitals.
  5. -- remains two separate hyphens in tńg--khì-o͘h.
  6. NFC, NFD, and Reverse Order render identically, and enabling letter-spacing does not break composition (hidden feature switches).

Why copy all three Unicode forms?

ButtonExample (ó͘)What it tests
Copy NFCU+00F3 ó + U+0358Standard precomposed form used by most websites and IMEs. Verifies topright anchors and ccmp rules on precomposed toned vowels.
Copy NFDU+006F o + U+0301 + U+0358Decomposed form produced by macOS file names and PDF copy-out. Verifies both marks attach cleanly without colliding.
Copy Reverse OrderU+006F o + U+0358 + U+0301Non-canonical mark order from sequential keyboards. Verifies engines and fonts that do not reorder marks by ccc.
